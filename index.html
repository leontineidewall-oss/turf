06 · faq

questions.

do coins need to sign up?

no. the map reads public pool data. any pump.fun coin that makes the top 12 by 24h volume is on it, whether it knows or not.

where do the numbers come from?

GeckoTerminal's public pool data for pump.fun's bonding curve and PumpSwap. each coin counts its busiest pool. last week's winner comes from daily candles, so anyone can check it.

who gets the pot?

wallets that hold at least the $TURF floor and at least the floor in dollars of the winning coin. the pot splits by $TURF bag across the top 180, and cuts under ◎0.001 are dropped.

when is it paid?

after the sunday close. the pot wallet signs the payout in batches from the pot room. every transfer shows on the pot's ledger with its transaction.

can it rug the pot?

the pot is a normal wallet and every move is public on the pot room ledger. there are no keys on this site: payouts are signed in the pot wallet's own app.

is $TURF a gang too?

yes. if $TURF trades hard enough to make the top 12, it fights for blocks like everyone else.
